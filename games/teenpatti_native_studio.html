TEEN PATTI VERSUS RULES

1. Objective: Bet on which 3-card hand (Player or Banker) will rank higher, or bet on a Tie.

2. Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest):

  • Trail / Trio: Three cards of same rank (AAA highest, 222 lowest).
  • Pure Sequence: 3 consecutive cards of same suit (AKQ suited highest).
  • Sequence: 3 consecutive cards of different suits (AKQ, KQJ).
  • Color / Flush: 3 cards of same suit not in sequence.
  • Pair: 2 cards of same rank.
  • High Card: Highest card among the 3.
