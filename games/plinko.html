HOW TO PLAY

1. Select your bet amount in PKR or USD using the stepper or quick chip buttons.

2. Select the number of balls to drop (1, 5, 10, or 25 balls).

3. Tap DROP BALL. The spinning drum tumbler selects the ball color and launches it into the 16-row pegboard pyramid.

4. When the ball lands in a bottom pocket, your payout is calculated: (Bet per Ball) × (Pocket Multiplier) × (Ball Multiplier) (Capped at 100X).
