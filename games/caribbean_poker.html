Caribbean Poker Rules

1. Placing Bets: Select chips and click ANTE to place your initial bet. Optionally click JACKPOT ($1) to participate in the Progressive Jackpot pool.

2. Dealing: Click Deal. You receive 5 face-up cards; the Dealer receives 4 face-down cards and 1 face-up card.

3. Play Decision:
• CALL: Place an additional bet equal to 2X your Ante to contest the hand.
• FOLD: Surrender your cards and forfeit your Ante bet.

4. Dealer Qualification: The Dealer must hold at least an Ace and a King, a pair, or better.
• If Dealer does not qualify: Ante pays 1:1, and your Call bet pushes (returned).
• If Dealer qualifies: Hands are compared. If you beat the dealer, Ante pays 1:1, and your Call bet pays according to the paytable!

5. Jackpot Side Bet: A Royal Flush with the Jackpot active wins 100% of the Progressive Pool! Straight Flushes win 10% of the pool.
