Game Rules

First Person XXXtreme Lightning Roulette is an electrifying roulette game. In each game round, yellow lightning strikes between 1 and 5 numbers, giving you the chance to win multiplied payouts from 50x up to 500x (49:1 to 499:1). In addition, red Chain Lightning can strike up to 10 total Lightning Numbers per round, and Double Strikes can boost existing multipliers up to 2000x (1999:1)!
